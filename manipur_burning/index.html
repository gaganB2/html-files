02 — The eruption
03 MAY 2023

Everything
caught fire.

A tribal solidarity march was followed by clashes that rapidly spread through multiple parts of the state. Homes burned. Communities fled. The conflict became one of India's most devastating episodes of recent ethnic violence.

A protest became a confrontation. A confrontation became a conflict. And a conflict became a catastrophe.
No graphic imagery. The absence is deliberate.
03 — The image that shook India
04 MAY

Two women.
A crowd.
A nation watching.

On 4 May 2023, two Kuki-Zo women were stripped and paraded by a mob in Manipur.

Images of the incident became public weeks later and provoked nationwide outrage. The Supreme Court described the violence against women as deeply disturbing and emphasised that women cannot be used as instruments of violence.

This section intentionally shows no graphic reconstruction. The event does not need spectacle to communicate its horror.

DOCUMENTED INCIDENT

The incident is part of court proceedings, investigations and extensive independent reporting. Survivors' dignity should remain central to how the event is remembered.

06 — The human cost
People
remember.

Families who left with almost nothing. Children growing up in relief camps. Villages that no longer look the way they did before May 2023.

There are no statistics capable of describing what it means to lose a home, a neighbour, a place of worship, or the feeling of safety.

07 — Unresolved

The story
is not finished.

The most important part of a documentary may be what it refuses to pretend is settled.

QUESTION 01

What happened to the investigations?

Thousands of cases entered the criminal justice system. The pace and outcome of investigations remain a central question.

QUESTION 02

How many people remain displaced?

Official and independent estimates differ. Rehabilitation and return remain major challenges.

QUESTION 03

Who answers for institutional failures?

The violence raised difficult questions about administration, policing, political leadership and accountability.

QUESTION 04

Can the communities live together again?

Beyond immediate security, the harder question concerns reconciliation, trust, land and the future of Manipur.
